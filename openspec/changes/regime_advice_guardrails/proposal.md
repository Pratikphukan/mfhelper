# Proposal: Make market-regime buy advice cautious and clear

## Problem

Market-regime advice currently appears beside existing BUY labels and calls to action without changing them. A BEAR warning can therefore sit beside a BUY label, and buy cards can show an amount multiplier based on a "normal top-up" the user does not have.

## Proposed change

Keep the technical dip signals as information, but make the regime determine how the email describes them. BEAR and CORRECTION signals should read as watch/review prompts. Capitulation wording should say that configured thresholds were met, not imply that a market bottom was confirmed. Remove amount suggestions until MFHelper has a real private investment limit.

## Outcome

The alert email will show what technical conditions were found and how the broad market is classified, without presenting an unbudgeted amount or implying that a dip is a guaranteed buying opportunity.
